Privacy Policy for Nova AI

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Nova AI application, published under the Google Play developer account ANAS, handles information. By using Nova AI, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information processed by the app

  • AI prompts and messages: Text that you enter is transmitted to an AI service provider so that the app can generate a response.
  • Device and app information: Advertising, analytics, security, and performance services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, crash information, diagnostics, operating-system version, and approximate location inferred from the IP address.
  • Support information: If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information included in your message.

2. How information is used

  • To provide AI-generated responses and operate the app.
  • To display, measure, and manage advertisements.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, fix errors, and understand app performance.
  • To respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Nova AI may display a limited number of advertisements. The app may use third-party services such as Google advertising, analytics, messaging, and related SDKs where enabled. These providers may process device and usage information according to their own privacy policies and applicable consent choices. AI prompts are also processed by the third-party AI provider used to generate responses.

Please do not enter highly sensitive personal information in AI prompts, including passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, private medical records, or other information you do not want processed by an external AI service.

4. Accounts

The current version of Nova AI does not require users to create an account. Account registration may be added in a future update. If that happens, this Privacy Policy and the app's data disclosures will be updated before or when the feature is introduced, and users will be provided with an account-deletion method as required.

5. Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the AI functionality, provide advertising and analytics, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, or comply with legal obligations. We do not intentionally sell the content of your AI prompts.

6. Data retention and deletion

Nova AI does not currently maintain user accounts. Information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. AI, advertising, analytics, and infrastructure providers may retain information for periods described in their own policies. Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain records, or meet legal requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the app. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

Nova AI is not specifically directed to children under 13. Users who are below the age required to consent to data processing in their country should use the app only with permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
